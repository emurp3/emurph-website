Privacy Policy

EL Murphy Enterprises (“we,” “us”) operates E Murph Radio and this website, emurph.com. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you give us — your name, phone number, and email address when you text the station, enter a giveaway, submit music, or contact us.
  • Messages you send us — texts and other messages sent to the E Murph Radio line (478-368-7742) or to our email addresses.
  • Giveaway and event information — details needed to run a promotion and deliver a prize, such as your name and a callback number.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your messages and requests.
  • To operate E Murph Radio listener communications, including song requests, shout-outs, and station updates you have opted in to receive.
  • To administer giveaways and notify winners.
  • To maintain the security of our services and comply with the law.

SMS / text messaging

When you opt in to receive texts from E Murph Radio (for example, by texting JOIN to 478-368-7742, texting the station line, signing up on our website, or accepting a personal invitation from the station), we use your mobile number only to send the station messages you agreed to receive. Message frequency is low and varies. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP, and get help by replying HELP.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate (such as our messaging and hosting providers), and only as needed for them to perform their work, or when required by law.

Your choices

You may opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP. You may ask us to correct or delete your personal information by contacting us at the address below.
