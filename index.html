About
EMurph

Eddie L. Murphy Jr. — known as EMurph — is a gospel hip-hop artist, educator, and minister of music from Macon, GA. His music sits at the intersection of faith, truth, and cultural witness.

With roots in the church and bars forged in real life, EMurph's sound is unafraid. He doesn't dress up hard truths — he brings them to the altar. Every track is a testimony.

Juneteenth: Crabs, Chains & Orange Kings is his boldest statement yet — 12 songs documenting freedom's cost, the traps we set for ourselves, and the sovereign kings God calls us to be.
